## <i class="fas fa-fw fa-microscope section-icon section-icon--research" aria-hidden="true"></i> Research
{: .section-heading .section-heading--research}

### Cross-Hemisphere Reconstruction-Derived Neuroanatomical Specificity in Schizophrenia

An ongoing multicenter structural MRI study of 948 participants across five sites that uses a pretrained Asy-GAN model to derive ANS and RNS measures of neuroanatomical specificity. The study evaluates multiscale group differences, clinical and cognitive associations, and schizophrenia classification using mixed-effects modeling, repeated cross-validation, leave-one-site-out validation, and permutation testing. ANS significantly outperformed conventional GMV asymmetry in matched classification analyses (Delta AUC = 0.086; FDR-adjusted q = 0.00051). The accompanying manuscript is **in preparation**.

### Cross-hemisphere reconstruction and handedness

Research in progress at the Cognomics Lab on whether cross-hemisphere reconstruction residuals capture distributed structural information related to handedness beyond conventional gray-matter asymmetry indices. The accompanying manuscript is **in preparation**.

### Operator-corrector validation of residual measures

An ongoing extension of the cross-hemisphere framework that evaluates operator-corrected residual measures across demographic, behavioral, and disease-related validation settings. This work is **in preparation**; detailed results will be shared after methodological and manuscript review.
