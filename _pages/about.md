I build reproducible research workflows and software for neuroimaging analysis, with an emphasis on interpretable validation, careful statistical testing, and tools that other researchers can install and reuse.

## Research

<span class="anchor" id="research"></span>

### Cross-hemisphere reconstruction and handedness

Research in progress at the Cognomics Lab on whether cross-hemisphere reconstruction residuals capture distributed structural information related to handedness beyond conventional gray-matter asymmetry indices. The accompanying manuscript is **in preparation**.

### Operator-corrector validation of residual measures

An ongoing extension of the cross-hemisphere framework that evaluates operator-corrected residual measures across demographic, behavioral, and disease-related validation settings. This work is **in preparation**; detailed results will be shared after methodological and manuscript review.
